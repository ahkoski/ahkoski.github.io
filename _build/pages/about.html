How I work

Six steps I follow on almost every project, whether it’s a course, a live session, or a product launch.

  1. 1

    Listen first

    I start with stakeholders, subject matter experts, and the people doing the work to find the real gap, not just the requested course.

    Example: Evaluating usage patterns and user needs before retiring an LMS at ZenGRC

  2. 2

    Map it out

    Every goal becomes an objective, and every objective gets an activity and a way to measure it. Then come the project plan and storyboards.

    Example: The design table behind Leading Through Change

  3. 3

    Make it playable

    People learn by doing, so I build in scenarios, games, and practice wherever I can.

    Example: Five mini-games in Adapting to Change

  4. 4

    Test with real people

    Pilots, reviewer feedback, and user testing catch problems before launch.

    Example: Pilot reviewers rating Adapting to Change “Very Effective”

  5. 5

    Launch and measure

    I track what matters, from completions and abandon rates to recommendation scores and adoption, and share what I learn with leaders.

    Example: A 43% lift in feature adoption at ZenGRC

  6. 6

    Make it repeatable

    Templates, guides, and frameworks let the next launch move faster.

    Example: A producer guide, handbook, and reusable video templates
