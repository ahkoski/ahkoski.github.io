The challenge

Legal assistants across the firm needed consistent, verifiable skills in the systems they used every day, from document management to time entry. Training was uneven across offices, and there was no objective way to measure proficiency.

My approach

One program, many partners

I worked with stakeholders from Accounting, IP, Technology, HR, and attorney leadership, and managed subject matter experts to keep complex content accurate.

Assessment people wanted to finish

I built a gamified assessment system with objective metrics to track success. The document management module, for example, is an adventure through the “land of DMS,” where learners show mastery by answering every question correctly.

Multiple ways to learn

Detailed training videos and eLearning modules were paired with a complementary series of live, interactive webinars, plus storyboarded demo videos used in both the program and new hire orientation.

Results

  • 300+ completions in the first year among 260+ legal assistants.
  • The document management module enrolled 125+ users in its first 3 months.
  • Became the firm’s standard for firm-wide training programs.
  • Strong reviews from users and leadership led to 7 additional projects requested by subject matter experts and department heads.
