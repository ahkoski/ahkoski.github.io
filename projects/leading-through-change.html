Leading Through Change & Transition

A highly interactive 60-minute virtual session that helps people leaders understand what their teams go through during change, and practice coaching them through it.

  • ~1,350supervisors and people leaders
  • 8.2/10average recommendation score
  • 60 minlive virtual session
My role
Designer and facilitator
Audience
Supervisors and above
Format
Live virtual session on Teams, breakout rooms of 5–7
Materials
Facilitator deck, participant handbook, producer guide

The challenge

Leaders were guiding their teams through stacked, continuous change, often before the last change had settled. Many knew change models in theory but struggled with the human side: reading where each person was, staying aligned with decisions they didn’t fully agree with, and knowing what to say in the moment.

My approach

Objectives mapped to activities

I built a design table that tied each program goal to course objectives, content, and an engagement or evaluation method. By the end, leaders could:

  • Tell the difference between change and transition
  • Explain the psychological transition process, including the impact of stacked or continuous change on teams
  • Evaluate the leader’s role in supporting employees through change
  • Apply practical techniques, including ADKAR and Circles of Control
  • Evaluate how vision, purpose, and aligned leader behavior affect engagement during change
  • Use Bridges’ Transition Model and questioning techniques to support teammates

Interactive at scale

The session ran live on Teams for large audiences, so engagement was built in from the first minute: an icebreaker on the attributes of change, live polls (“How many active changes is your team navigating right now?”), a Circles of Control quick sort, and a closing commitment to one action.

Coaching practice with realistic personas

In breakout rooms of 5–7, leaders worked through scenarios built around four employee personas, each at a different point in transition. For each one, they identified the stage, chose questions to learn more, and planned a way forward. Debrief slides modeled strong questions and responses.

Ready for other facilitators

A participant handbook, a technical producer guide, and an LMS course description made the session repeatable.

Results

  • Reached approximately 1,350 supervisors and people leaders.
  • Earned an average recommendation score of 8.2/10.
  • Participants consistently highlighted the relevance, practicality, interactivity, and immediate application to real leadership situations.
