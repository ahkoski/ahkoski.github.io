The challenge

The product team shipped new features regularly, but adoption lagged. Customers didn’t always discover what was new, and sales and support teams weren’t consistently trained before launch. Learning content lived in a legacy LMS that was costly and disconnected from where customers actually spent time.

My approach

Teach inside the product

I designed Pendo in-app guides for new-customer onboarding and added feature badges that pointed people to what was new, right where they worked.

Make training easy to fit in

I replaced long sessions with microlearning and asynchronous training for go-to-market teams, ran bi-weekly webinars and demos on new features and their value, and moved the feature adoption program to parallel training tracks instead of a sequential handoff.

Build a home for learning

I launched ZenGRC University on SkillJar for customers, employees, and partners, then later retired the legacy LMS and moved content into the customer community without interrupting customers. I also took ownership of the knowledge base, created a Solutions Center for GRC best practices, and added learning pages with videos and guided onboarding content.

Close the loop with customers

I set up and led a cross-departmental Customer Impact Team for weekly user acceptance testing and customer feedback review, and served on the Product Advisory Board.

Results

  • 43% increase in feature engagement from in-app feature badges.
  • Go-to-market training participation grew from 21% to 100%.
  • Feature time-to-market shortened by 2 weeks.
  • 4,000+ customers, employees, and partners enrolled in ZenGRC University.
  • Knowledge base: 65% and 74% rises in views of top product videos, 56% more help content views, a 30% increase in customer helpfulness scores, and 30% growth in community visits.
  • Helped launch the customer community, which attracted 6,000+ users.
  • Trained and tested the company’s AI support chatbot with developers, improving its answers by finding knowledge gaps and strengthening the knowledge base content behind it.
  • Retiring the legacy LMS saved the company more than $33,000 a year.
